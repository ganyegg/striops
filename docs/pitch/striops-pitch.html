The product — defined

What exactly is Striops?

Striops is a Strategic Intelligence Operating System: a continuously updated, source-linked model of the city — its money, water, roads, safety, and services — with reasoning engines on top that turn raw movement into risks, opportunities, and recommended decisions, delivered as a daily executive brief.

It watches

Live departmental feeds, Treasury returns, open data — ingested continuously, not compiled quarterly.

It verifies

Every number carries a resolvable public source and a verification badge. Defensible in council, in media, in court.

It reasons

Forecasting, risk scoring, opportunity detection and simulation engines — explainable arithmetic, not black-box magic.

It remembers

A decision register that links every choice to its owner, its risk, and its review date — memory that survives turnover.

One sentence: Striops is the strategic twin of Cape Town — the city, modelled, watched, and argued over by software that shows its work.

The trust principle

Every number can defend itself.

Click any figure in Striops and it opens its own report: the trend, the score arithmetic, and the public source it came from — a budget book page, a Treasury return, a DWS bulletin, a SAPS quarter.

Verified

Cross-checked against the cited public source. Quote it in council without flinching.

Needs verification

Sourced but awaiting re-check — Striops says so on the face of the number instead of hiding it.

Estimate

Modelled or derived, with the method attached. Honest uncertainty beats confident fiction.

When a journalist asks "where does that number come from?", the answer is a link — not a scramble.

"Our data is sensitive."

Striops runs inside the City's own cloud tenancy if required. POPIA compliance is a design constraint, not a patch. The pilot's MOU defines exactly what is accessed, by whom, and for what.

"AI makes things up."

In Striops, AI writes prose — never numbers. Scores and forecasts are deterministic arithmetic you can audit. Kill the AI layer entirely and every figure still stands.

"Integrations always slip."

That is why the pilot is fixed-price with a binary success test. Integration risk is ours. If day 90 arrives without a live, uncompiled brief, the pilot fee is the end of your exposure.

"What if you disappear?"

Data stays in your tenancy; exports are open formats; the seed and provenance registers are yours. No hostage-taking. Continuity terms are in the contract, not in goodwill.
